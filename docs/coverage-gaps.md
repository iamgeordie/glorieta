# Coverage gaps found against commercial practice tests

Glorieta is checked against commercial Spanish theory practice tests. Those tests are a topic guide only: when one asks about something Glorieta does not teach, we write the lesson from the law (see [`learn-authoring.md`](learn-authoring.md)), never from the practice test. The full record of each test (questions, answers, per-question cross-reference) is kept privately in Landru, not in this public repo.

## Source: Facilauto "Examen por ordenador B" block, all 61 tests (1,830 questions, 2026-11-18)

After five passes, 1,807 of the 1,830 questions (98.7%) are taught: 961 were already covered by the time each was reviewed, and 846 are covered by new lessons and checks. Over the five passes the course went from 65 to 90 lessons and from 335 to 1,027 checks, and the sign gallery from 128 to 197 signs (since grown to the whole catalogue). Every new check carries an Exam English block. No check shares a six-word run with the practice bank's questions or options.

The fifth pass drew on the DGT's own training manuals: Manual VIII *Mecánica* (2026), Manual II *Cuestiones de seguridad vial* (2026) and the *Manual del permiso B en lectura fácil* (2023). Where a manual and the law in force disagree, Glorieta teaches the law.

### Where the practice test disagrees with the law in force

Glorieta teaches the law. On these items the practice bank's "correct" answer is out of date or wrong, so a learner may see a different answer there. In the app, each one is a "Law changed" or "Watch out" box next to the rule, and the Tricks and Tips screen lists them all:

- **Helmets:** moped helmets are required on every road (RGC art. 118.1.a, unchanged since 2006), not only outside towns.
- **Footwear and gloves:** since 2026-10-01 closed footwear on every road and gloves on intercity roads are a legal duty for riders (RGC art. 118.1.b and c), not just advice.
- **Riding two abreast:** riding two abreast on the shoulder is allowed for bicycles only (RGC art. 36.2), not mopeds.
- **Shoulder in jams:** a motorcycle may use a motorway shoulder in a jam only on signed stretches, with traffic stopped, at up to 30 km/h (RGC art. 36.3).
- **Warning triangles:** they stopped counting as the V-16 signal on 2026-01-01.
- **Animals:** animals have priority only on a signed livestock route (cañada), not at every domestic-animals warning sign.
- **Seat belts:** taxi drivers have no in-town seat-belt exemption in the article in force (RGC art. 119). The DGT's 2026 Manual II still lists it.
- **Head restraint:** it is set by the back and top of the head (DGT), not with its centre at eye level.
- **Cyclists' position:** cyclists ride preferably in the centre of the lane under the law in force, not at the right edge.
- **Towing:** no article in force covers towing a broken-down car on a motorway; the RGC and RGV rules were deleted in 2021. The DGT's 2026 manuals say only a roadside assistance vehicle may tow it, and Glorieta teaches that as DGT guidance.
- **Children on bicycles:** since 2026-10-01 an adult rider may carry a child of up to 22 kg who can sit unaided, in an approved extra seat (RGC art. 154.2); the bank still uses an age limit.
- **Overtaking pedestrians:** the 1.5 m gap when overtaking pedestrians applies in towns too (RGC art. 85.4).
- **Town-entry sign:** the limit on a town-entry sign covers that road only (Catalogue, S-500), not the whole town.
- **Level crossings:** RGC art. 87.1.b has no exception for two-wheelers.
- **Insurance:** compulsory insurance pays the victims of a drunk or uninsured driver, and the insurer then claims the money from the driver (RDL 8/2004 art. 10.a).
- **Driving licence:** it is shown physically or digitally; a certified photocopy is not an accepted form. The law allows certified copies only of the vehicle documents (RGV art. 26.2).
- **Unticketed parking:** a car parked without a ticket in a time-limited zone may be towed away (LSV art. 105.1.g).
- **First aid:** for a person who is not breathing, the DGT's 2026 first-aid manual calls for chest compressions rather than artificial respiration alone.
