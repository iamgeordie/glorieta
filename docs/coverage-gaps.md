# Coverage gaps found against commercial practice tests

Glorieta is checked against commercial Spanish theory practice tests. Those tests are a topic guide only: when one asks about something Glorieta does not teach, we write the lesson from the law (see [`learn-authoring.md`](learn-authoring.md)), never from the practice test. The full record of each test (questions, answers, per-question cross-reference) is kept privately in Landru, not in this public repo.

## Source: Facilauto "Examen por ordenador B" block, all 61 tests (1,830 questions, 2026-11-18)

After four passes, 1,744 of the 1,830 questions (95.3%) are taught: 960 were already covered by the time each was reviewed, and 784 are covered by new lessons and checks. Over the four passes the course went from 65 to 87 lessons and from 335 to 965 checks, and the sign gallery from 128 to 194 signs. Every new check carries an Exam English block. No check shares a six-word run with the practice bank's questions or options.

### Where the practice test disagrees with the law in force

Glorieta teaches the law. On these items the practice bank's "correct" answer is out of date or wrong, so a learner may see a different answer there:

- **Helmets:** moped helmets are required on every road since 2026-10-01 (RGC art. 118.1.a), not only outside towns.
- **Footwear:** closed footwear for riders is a legal duty, not just advice.
- **Riding two abreast:** riding two abreast on the shoulder is allowed for bicycles only (RGC art. 36.2), not mopeds.
- **Shoulder in jams:** a motorcycle may use a motorway shoulder in a jam only on signed stretches, with traffic stopped, at up to 30 km/h (RGC art. 36.3).
- **Warning triangles:** they stopped counting as the V-16 signal on 2026-01-01.
- **Animals:** animals have priority only on a signed livestock route (cañada), not at every domestic-animals warning sign.
- **Seat belts:** taxi drivers have no in-town seat-belt exemption in the article in force (RGC art. 119).
- **Head restraint:** it is set by the back and top of the head (DGT), not at eye level.
- **Cyclists' position:** cyclists ride preferably in the centre of the lane under the law in force, not at the right edge.
- **Towing:** towing a broken-down car on a motorway is no longer regulated in RGC art. 130; the rule the bank cites was deleted in 2021.
- **Overtaking pedestrians:** the 1.5 m gap when overtaking pedestrians applies in towns too (RGC art. 85.4).
- **Town-entry sign:** the limit on a town-entry sign covers that road only (Catalogue, S-500), not the whole town.
- **Level crossings:** RGC art. 87.1.b has no exception for two-wheelers.
- **Insurance:** compulsory insurance pays the victims of a drunk or uninsured driver, and the insurer then claims the money from the driver (RDL 8/2004 art. 10.a).
- **Driving licence:** it is shown physically or digitally; a certified photocopy is not an accepted form.
- **Unticketed parking:** a car parked without a ticket in a time-limited zone may be towed away (LSV art. 105.1.g).
- **First aid:** for a person who is not breathing, the DGT's 2026 first-aid manual calls for chest compressions rather than artificial respiration alone.
