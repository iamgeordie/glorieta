# Coverage gaps found against commercial practice tests

Topics that a commercial Spanish theory practice test asked about and Glorieta does not yet teach, or teaches only in part. Each entry names the topic in our own words and the lesson that should own it. Write the content from the law (see [`learn-authoring.md`](learn-authoring.md)), never from the practice test.

The full record of each test (screens, questions, answers) is kept privately in Landru, not in this public repo.

## Source: Facilauto test 041 (2026-11-18)

13 of 30 questions covered, 7 partly covered, 10 not covered.

### Not covered

| Topic | Suggested home |
|---|---|
| Lateral load overhang on two-wheelers (0.50 m each side of the longitudinal axis, vehicles under 1 m wide) | `equipment/kit-and-load` |
| S-series indication signs: S-7 recommended maximum speed, S-26a/b/c exit approach panels, S-126 parking for bus users | `signs.json` and the signs module; S-7 also in `speed` |
| Driving astride longitudinal lines is banned; exception for lanes under 3 m wide | `signs/road-markings` |
| Maximum vehicle height: 4 m general, 4.20 m urban class I buses, 4.50 m car transporters, recovery vehicles and container carriers | `equipment/kit-and-load` |
| Engine systems and fault symptoms, e.g. higher fuel consumption points to the ignition or fuel supply system | `equipment/upkeep` |
| Preventive driving (conducción preventiva) as a named attitude | `hazards/look-anticipate-decide` |
| Who is liable for an offence: the perpetrator; parents or guardians jointly for a minor; the driver for passengers without helmets | `docs/offences` |
| Social and economic cost of road accidents | new step in `safety` or `hazards` |
