# Coverage gaps found against commercial practice tests

Glorieta is checked against commercial Spanish theory practice tests. Those tests are a topic guide only: when one asks about something Glorieta does not teach, we write the lesson from the law (see [`learn-authoring.md`](learn-authoring.md)), never from the practice test. The full record of each test (questions, answers, per-question cross-reference) is kept privately in Landru, not in this public repo.

## Source: Facilauto "Examen por ordenador B" block, tests 1–21 and 41 (660 questions, 2026-11-18)

After two passes, 646 of the 660 questions are taught: 257 were already covered, and 389 are covered by new lessons and checks. The course went from 65 to 83 lessons and from 335 to 651 checks. 26 S-series indication signs joined the gallery. Every new check carries an Exam English block.

Tests 22–40 and 42–61 of the block have not been reviewed yet.

### Where the practice test disagrees with the law in force

Glorieta teaches the law. These items in the practice bank are out of date or wrong, so a learner may see a different "correct" answer there:

- Moped helmets: required on every road since 2026-10-01 (RGC art. 118.1.a), not only outside towns.
- Closed footwear for riders is a legal duty, not just advice.
- Riding two abreast on the shoulder is allowed for bicycles only (RGC art. 36.2), not mopeds.
- A motorcycle may use a motorway shoulder in a jam only on signed stretches, with traffic stopped, at up to 30 km/h (RGC art. 36.3).
- Warning triangles stopped counting as the V-16 signal on 2026-01-01.
- Animals have priority only on a signed livestock route (cañada), not at every domestic-animals warning sign.
